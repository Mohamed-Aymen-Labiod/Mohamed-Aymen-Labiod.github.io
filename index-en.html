I am an Associate Professor (MCF) at Université Paris Est Créteil (UPEC) since September 2020, in the Networks and Telecommunications department of IUT de Créteil/Vitry. My research is conducted within the LISSI Lab (EA 3956), in the CIR team (Intelligent Controls in Networks).

I hold an M.Sc. in Telecommunications, Networks and Multimedia (valedictorian). I completed a joint international PhD entitled "Contribution to the improvement of video transmissions in vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs)", carried out at IEMN-DOAE (UMR CNRS 8520) under the supervision of Prof. François-Xavier Coudoux and Dr. Mohamed Gharbi. The defense took place in July 2019 at Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France. I then held two ATER positions — at INSA Hauts-de-France (Institut National des Sciences Appliquées Hauts-de-France) and at IUT de Valenciennes (2018–2020) — before joining UPEC.

Research Interests

  • Dynamic, Vehicular Networks & eHealth IoT: SDN/NFV architectures for connected ambulances and vehicular networks (V2X), 5G-NTN, network slicing, scheduling, cross-layer approaches, Edge Computing (collaboration with CHU Henri Mondor, AP-HP)
  • 5G/6G Network Security: blockchain, collaborative AI, intrusion detection for IoT and V2X
  • Quality of Experience (QoE) via Affective Computing: EEG/ECG signals, multi-task LSTM deep learning, closed-loop network control
  • Video Transmission & Encoding: adaptive streaming, H.265/HEVC encoding, cross-layer optimization for telemedicine and vehicular communications
  • Deepfake & Synthetic Content Detection: identification and attribution of AI-generated images, benchmark datasets and models
  • Agriculture 4.0: digital twins, wireless sensor networks, deep learning for precision irrigation and fertilization
  • Experimental Platforms: Open Air Interface (OAI), 4G/5G deployments, GNU Radio, SDR

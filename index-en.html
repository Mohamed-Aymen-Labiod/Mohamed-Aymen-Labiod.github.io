After receiving an M.Sc. degree in Telecommunications, Networks and Multimedia (valedictorian) from Badji Mokhtar University (Annaba, Algeria, 2015) — with a thesis defense at the Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France (UPHF) following a six-month fellowship — I began a joint PhD in January 2016. Entitled "Contribution to the improvement of video transmissions in vehicular ad-hoc networks (VANETs)", the work was conducted at IEMN-DOAE (UMR CNRS 8520) under the supervision of Prof. François-Xavier Coudoux and Dr. Mohamed Gharbi, in co-supervision with LASA Lab (Badji Mokhtar University). Defense: July 5, 2019, Valenciennes.

After two ATER positions (UPHF 2018–2019 and IUT de Valenciennes 2019–2020), I joined Université Paris Est Créteil (UPEC) in September 2020 as an Associate Professor (MCF) in the Networks and Telecommunications department of IUT de Créteil/Vitry. My research at the LISSI Lab (EA 3956), within the CIR team led by Prof. Abdelhamid Mellouk, focuses on high-dynamic networks, affective computing-driven QoE prediction, and smart agriculture (Agriculture 4.0).

Research Interests

  • Dynamic Networks & eHealth IoT: SDN/NFV architectures for connected ambulances, 5G-NTN networks, Edge Computing (collaboration with CHU Henri Mondor, AP-HP)
  • Quality of Experience (QoE) via Affective Computing: EEG/ECG signals, multi-task LSTM deep learning, closed-loop network control
  • Agriculture 4.0: digital twins, wireless sensor networks, deep learning for precision irrigation and fertilization
  • 5G/6G Network Security: blockchain, collaborative AI, intrusion detection for IoT and V2X
  • Experimental Platforms: Open Air Interface (OAI), 4G/5G deployments, GNU Radio, SDR

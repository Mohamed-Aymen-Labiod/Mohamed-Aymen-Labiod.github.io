Plateforme 5G (Open Air Interface)
Mohamed-Aymen Labiod (porteur principal)

Deployment of a 5G platform based on software-defined radio and the Open Air Interface (OAI) solution. This flexible test environment enables evaluation and validation of approaches for 4G and 5G networks under near-real conditions: real-time network parameter variation, advanced traffic monitoring, and IoT platform integration.

5G / 4G Open Air Interface GNU Radio SDR NFV

Plateforme Réseaux multi-accès
Tuan Chu, Mohamed-Aymen Labiod, Abdelhamid Mellouk

Design and implementation of a multi-access network deployment platform in various urban mobile environments. The solution has been tested in real scenarios: urban, metro, suburban rail, vehicular, etc. It enables evaluation and validation of scheduling and mobility management approaches in heterogeneous contexts.

Multi-access SDN Mobilité urbaine IoT e-santé

Plateforme d'évaluation de la QoE par signaux physiologiques
Dr. Yann Senova (CHU Mondor), Mohamed-Aymen Labiod, Abdelhamid Mellouk

Multimodal platform developed in collaboration with the neurosurgery team at CHU Henri Mondor (AP-HP). It integrates physiological data — EEG, ECG, galvanic skin response — to predict and assess Quality of Experience (QoE) in human interactions with multimedia services. The processing pipeline extracts spectral features (differential entropy, power spectral density) feeding a multi-task LSTM model.

EEG / ECG QoE LSTM Informatique affective CHU Mondor

Plateforme de migration de services définis par logiciel (SDSM)
Tuan Chu, Mohamed-Aymen Labiod, Brice Augustin

Platform based on the Software-Defined Service Migration (SDSM) paradigm in Multi-Access Edge Computing (MEC) environments over 5G networks. It uses a centralized SDN controller and the QUIC protocol to optimize service migration, ensuring continuity even under dynamic network conditions (mobility, instability). Container and connection migration is anticipated to reduce perceived latency.

MEC SDN QUIC 5G Migration de services

DeepGuardDB — Dataset images réelles et synthétiques
Reghioua, Namani, Bendiab, Labiod, Shiaeles · IEEE Dataport, 2024

Dataset published on IEEE Dataport comprising real images and AI-generated synthetic images (text-to-image). DeepGuardDB was designed to facilitate research on the detection and attribution of AI-generated content, particularly in the context of combating deepfakes and fabricated images.
